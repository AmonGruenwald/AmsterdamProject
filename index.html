🚲 Amsterdam Simulator

Cycle the canal ring. Collect stroopwafels. Ring your bell at tourists drifting into the bike lane. Catch a bus, if the QR scanner is in the mood. Eat herring, patat, and kroketten from the wall — you burn real calories out here, and what goes in must come out, so learn where the krul urinoirs are. Rent a sloop at a BOOT VERHUUR dock and narrate a canal tour for three tourists who believed the brochure. Visit De Wallen, respectfully; slow down and it may wink back — it has its own soundtrack now. And when the rain gets philosophical, duck into Koffieshop De Slang, where the city folds into an older, flatter Amsterdam. Mind the canals — the water accepts all bikes, and it will accept yours.
